# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Perch community a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion,
or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for
different viewpoints, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is best
for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, personal or political attacks, public or private harassment,
publishing another person's private information without permission, and other
conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards. They may remove, edit, or reject contributions and ban contributors
whose behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to **conduct@perch.app**. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and will explain
enforcement decisions to affected participants when safe and appropriate.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers use four levels proportionate to impact and recurrence:

1. **Correction** — private explanation and requested correction.
2. **Warning** — warning with consequences for continued behavior.
3. **Temporary ban** — time-limited suspension from project interaction.
4. **Permanent ban** — permanent removal from project spaces.
